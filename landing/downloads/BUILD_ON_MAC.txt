MirrorZ-Hecras macOS DMG — build required

This cloud/Linux environment cannot produce a runnable Mac .dmg
(PyInstaller freezes the host OS; hdiutil is macOS-only).

On your Mac, from the cloned repo:
  bash packaging/build_macos_dmg.sh

That writes:
  ~/Downloads/MirrorZ-Hecras-0.4.0.dmg
  landing/downloads/MirrorZ-Hecras-0.4.0.dmg
  releases/MirrorZ-Hecras-0.4.0.dmg

Then open landing/index.html — Download for Mac works.
